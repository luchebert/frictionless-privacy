What Frictionless is

A short overview for anyone trying the app — no technical background needed.

Frictionless is a private journal for habits and energy. It helps you notice what got in the way and how you felt, not whether you “kept a streak.”

Skipping is allowed. Logging is optional. Nothing in the app scolds you, scores you, or sends your notes to us. Your entries stay on your phone unless you choose to export them.

What it is not

  • Not a streak app, leaderboard, or calorie counter
  • Not medical advice or therapy
  • Not a social network — there is no feed and no account to create

The four tabs

  • Today — Check in on energy (1–5). For each habit, you can say you did it or skipped. If you skipped, you can tap reasons (tired, out of time, and so on). If you did it, you can mark the full version or a smaller “survival minimum,” and optionally how much energy it took versus what it gave back.
  • Insights — Pictures of patterns over time: energy, skip reasons, a private weekly note, and a shareable “receipt” that shows totals only (no habit names).
  • Habits — Add or pause the things you want to track. You can set a tiny backup version for hard days, and optional reminders that are a nudge, not a deadline.
  • Experiments — Try one small change for a week or two. The app may suggest a gentler version if your logs show something has been draining. You can ignore suggestions. Stopping an experiment does not count against you.

Privacy in one sentence

Your journal lives on your device. We do not run a login or analytics. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Who it is for

People who want a calmer way to notice habits — especially if streak apps have felt harsh. Testers helping before the public Play Store listing: please also read How to test.
