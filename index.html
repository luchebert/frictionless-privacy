Privacy Policy

Frictionless (package app.frictionless.mobile)
Effective 11 September 2026 · Last updated 11 September 2026

Canonical URL: luchebert.github.io/frictionless-privacy

Frictionless is a local-first habit and energy tracker. This policy describes what the app stores, what it does not send, and what happens when you choose to export or share.

Who we are

Frictionless is published by Luc Hébert. Contact: luc.hebert@protonmail.com.

What the app stores (on your device only)

All of the following stay in a local SQLite database on the phone or tablet. We do not operate an account system, and the app does not upload this data to our servers.

  • Habit names, icons, pause/archive state, optional survival minimums, and typical energy cost/return
  • Daily logs (done or skipped), optional notes, skip reasons, optional skip cost, optional post-action energy cost/return
  • Daily energy check-ins and weekly reflection notes
  • Experiments, hypotheses, and optional daily check-ins
  • Reminder preferences (on/off, times, optional notification copy)
  • Onboarding and similar local flags

What we do not collect

  • No sign-in, email, or profile in the app
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs in the product
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access for the core features
  • No remote or push-notification service: reminders are scheduled locally on the device

Notifications

Optional AM/PM cues and per-habit timed anchors use the operating system’s local notification APIs. Copies you write stay on device. We do not send remote push messages.

Sharing and export (only when you choose)

Nothing leaves the device unless you use an export or share action:

  • JSON backup — a file you save or send; it contains your habits, logs, and related local data so you can restore on another device.
  • Markdown review — a readable file of recent logs, energy, and weekly notes.
  • Week receipt — an image of aggregates only (counts and energy). It does not include habit names or private reflection text.

Those flows use the system share sheet or a file you pick. The destination (Messages, Drive, email, etc.) is under your control. We do not receive a copy.

Restore and deletion

You can import a backup you previously exported. Settings includes Clear all data, which deletes local habits, logs, notes, experiments, and settings on that device and returns the app to first-run. Uninstalling the app also removes the local database unless the OS backs it up by its own rules.

Children

Frictionless is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Google Play and third-party platforms

If you install from Google Play, Google may process install, device, and account data under Google’s policies. That processing is not performed by Frictionless. Development and store builds may be produced with Expo Application Services; those tools receive the app binary for build/submit, not your in-app journal.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
