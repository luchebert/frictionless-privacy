How to test Frictionless

A simple plan for friends and Play testers. You do not need to know how apps are built. Please read the overview first if the app is new to you.

If you were invited through Google Play

  1. Open the invite link Luc sent while signed into the Google account he added.
  2. Tap Become a tester.
  3. Install from the Play Store (not a file someone emailed). If you already installed a test copy some other way, uninstall that first.
  4. Open Frictionless at least once so Play knows you actually used it.
  5. Stay a tester for 14 days or more. Don’t tap “Leave the test” in Play. Google needs a group of people to stay opted in the whole time.

You can use the app lightly after that — a few opens over two weeks is enough. Daily use is welcome, not required.

First open (about 5 minutes)

  1. Walk through the welcome screens if they appear.
  2. On Today, set today’s energy (any number is fine).
  3. Add one small habit if the list is empty — something tiny, like “stretch” or “drink water.”
  4. Optional: give it a survival minimum (the smallest version that still counts on a hard day).
  5. Tap the habit and log I did it or Skipped. There is no wrong answer.

Try over a few days (pick any of these)

  • Log again tomorrow. Change your mind and clear a log if you want — that should work.
  • Skip once and tap a reason. See if Insights starts to show a pattern.
  • Write a short weekly note on Insights if you feel like it (it stays private).
  • On Experiments, start a 7-day experiment or ignore suggestions. Stop it early if you want.
  • Open Settings (gear). You do not need to export anything. If you do export, you choose where the file goes.
  • Turn a reminder on or leave reminders off. They are optional.

What “good” looks like

  • The app opens and doesn’t freeze.
  • You never feel forced to keep a streak.
  • Skipping and pausing feel normal, not like failure.
  • Your notes are still there after you close the app.

What to tell Luc

A short message is perfect. Helpful details:

  • Phone type (for example Pixel or Samsung) and roughly which Android version if you know it
  • What you tapped, and what you expected
  • Anything confusing, broken, or that felt guilt-y (that would be a bug in the spirit of the app)

Email: luc.hebert@protonmail.com

You can skip

  • You don’t need to fill every screen or “complete” the app
  • You don’t need to understand databases, builds, or the Play Console
  • You don’t need to share screenshots of your private notes
